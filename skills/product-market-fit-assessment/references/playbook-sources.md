| Acquisition | Channel portfolio, qualified acquisition, incrementality, marginal economics, saturation, and market entry |
| Metrics and Measurement | Metric contracts, cohort construction, causal boundaries, definitions, reconciliation, and evidence maturity |
| Growth Model | Connecting fit, lifecycle value, channels, loops, economics, reinvestment, and constraints |
| Product-Market Fit Cases | Dropbox, Facebook, HubSpot, Eventbrite, and Lovable evidence patterns and transfer boundaries |

Use these sources for conceptual and case grounding. They do not replace current product evidence, internal definitions, customer permission, local market review, or causal validation.

# Growth Playbook Sources

Pinned repository commit: `b2ae91400ec280479ca31903bf90534ca95a9b18`

| Playbook area | Enablement use | Source text |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Positioning | Buying situations, alternatives, value and proof chains, qualification, narrative, objections, sales progression, claims, and journey alignment |
| Customer Segmentation & ICP | Decision-changing segments, reachability, prerequisites, exclusions, buying path, retained value, and economics |
| Acquisition | Sales-led and partner-led mechanisms, promise and entry continuity, channel-model fit, qualified progression, downstream quality, and economics |
| Monetization | Offer, packaging, pricing, value metric, discounting, commercial fit, contribution, implementation cost, and expansion |
| Growth Infrastructure | Shared evidence, decision rights, workflow, delivery, governance, feedback, versioning, and build-versus-buy boundaries |
| Experimentation | Assignment, exposure, interference, metric quality, maturity, guardrails, and causal decisions |

These sources provide enablement-system principles, not the user's account facts, buying motives, relationship, private product or competitive information, approved claims, prices, sales process, CRM access, performance, market conditions, or causal results.

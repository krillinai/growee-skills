# Growth Playbook Sources

## Versioned Handbook Basis

Use these Growth Playbook pages at commit `b2ae914`. The pin keeps definitions and reasoning reviewable as the Playbook evolves.

- Metrics: metric roles, contracts, trees, cohorts, quality, North Star candidates, guardrails, business health, and governance.
- Growth Model: connection among customer value, lifecycle, funnels, loops, monetization, constraints, and reinvestment.
- Funnel & Constraint: value states, denominators, transitions, absolute contribution, and constraint diagnosis.
- Experimentation: causal evidence, assignment, maturity, uncertainty, interference, guardrails, and decision rules.
- Growth Infrastructure: instrumentation, identity, data quality, lineage, experimentation platforms, privacy, and operating controls.
- Retention: recurring value, natural intervals, mature cohorts, churn, resurrection, and retained quality.

These chapters provide transferable definitions and questions. They do not verify a private metric, source, baseline, target, benchmark, causal relationship, or result.
